Refuse a [dashboard] the relay must not run.

The allowlist is required in every admission mode. In open mode in particular, any daemon can register, and without it every one of them would get a trusted HTTPS host under this relay’s domain - free hosting for whatever its gateway serves, under the operator’s name.
